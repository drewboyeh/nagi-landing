## Disclosure

Any paid, gifted, or commissioned post must disclose the relationship clearly and
conspicuously, per FTC endorsement guidelines.

- `#ad` or `#sponsored` **at the start** of the caption, not buried after a "more" cut
- Verbal disclosure in video content as well — captions alone are not sufficient when the
  claim is spoken
- "Gifted" is a material connection and must be disclosed even with no payment

## Approved language

Use these freely. They describe what the product does to hair and how it feels, which is
exactly what a cosmetic is allowed to claim.

**Hair:** cleanses · washes · removes buildup · clarifies · purifies · hydrates ·
moisturizes · conditions · softens · smooths · detangles · defines curls · reduces frizz ·
adds shine · adds volume · adds texture · "leaves my hair feeling…"

**Scalp — cleanser only:** cleanses the scalp · removes buildup · "leaves my scalp feeling
clean / refreshed / balanced" · lightweight · non-stripping

## The simple test

Describe **how your hair looks and feels.** Never describe **a condition it fixed.**

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "my scalp feels clean without that stripped feeling" | "cleared up my flaky scalp" |
| "way less frizz in humidity" | "repaired my damaged hair" |
| "my curls actually hold definition" | "helped my hair grow" |
| "no buildup even on day three" | "soothed my itchy scalp" |

## Personal experience is not an exemption

"It worked for my dandruff" is still a drug claim when it appears on a post we paid for.
Sincerity does not change the regulatory reading. This is the single most common way brands
in this category get into trouble — a creator describes a real, genuine result, and the
brand is liable for it.

## Why we are strict about this

We are a cosmetic brand. Cosmetics need no FDA approval, which is why we can exist at our
size. Drugs require a monograph active, a Drug Facts panel, an NDC number and a registered
drug establishment. A single caption can move us across that line retroactively.

We would rather over-explain this once than pull a post.
