# Content Calendar — 8 Weeks to Founding Batch

28 posts, 3–4 per week. Built to run while the product is still being made, so
posting starts immediately and does not wait on anything.

**Sequencing rule:** weeks 1–4 need no finished product. Weeks 5–8 use batch
photos and results. If the formula slips, the early weeks still run.

**Every post routes to the quiz**, not to a product page. The quiz is
interactive, it ends with a personalised answer, and that is the moment someone
will hand over an email. `quiz.html` captures it with their scalp type, climate
and recommended routine attached.

**Every caption must pass `npm run compliance`.** The vocabulary in
`COMPLIANCE.md` applies to social copy exactly as it applies to the site — a
caption is a claim. Draft captions in a file and run the linter before posting.

Platform: shoot once, post to Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Optimise for Instagram.

---

## The four pillars

| Pillar | Why it earns reach | Share of calendar |
|---|---|---|
| **Process / making** | High completion — people stay to see the result | ~35% |
| **Ingredient education** | High saves, the strongest algorithmic signal | ~30% |
| **Contrarian takes** | High comments | ~20% |
| **Behind the business** | Builds the parasocial pull that converts to loyalty | ~15% |

# WEEK 1 — Establish what this is

### 1. Series opener · Process · no product
**Hook:** "I'm making a hair product from scratch. This is day one."
**Substance:** Rice, water, a jar. Say what you're making and that you'll show
every step including the parts that fail. Sets the expectation that this account
is a build, not an ad.
**CTA:** "Following along? The routine quiz is in my bio."

### 2. Why the salt came out · Contrarian · no product
**Hook:** "I took the salt out of my curl spray. On purpose."
**Substance:** Salt pulls moisture out of the strand, which lifts the cuticle.
That lifted, roughened surface is exactly what gives you grit and separation —
and it is the opposite of what defined curls need, which is a cuticle lying
flat so clumps hold and light reflects. Beach texture and curl definition are
opposite mechanisms. You cannot get both from one bottle.
**CTA:** Quiz link.
**Note:** Strong opener because it contradicts an entire product category.

### 3. The whole formula · Education · no product
**Hook:** "Nine ingredients. That's the entire formula."
**Substance:** Read the list. Say what each one is for in four words. The point
is not that nine is impressive — it is that you can name the job of every single
one, which most brands cannot because they did not choose them.
**Format:** Carousel, one ingredient per card. Built to be saved.

# WEEK 2 — Earn credibility

### 4. The preservative take · Contrarian · no product
**Hook:** "'Preservative-free' on a water-based product is a red flag, not a
selling point."
**Substance:** Water plus plant material plus a warm bathroom grows bacteria and
mould. A preservative is the ingredient that stops your product becoming a
science experiment between uses. Brands market its absence because it sounds
pure. It is the single most pro-consumer ingredient in the bottle.
**CTA:** Quiz link.
**Note:** Expect argument in the comments. That is the point — argument is reach.

### 5. Rice water, start to finish · Process · no product
**Hook:** "This is what a thousand years of hair care looks like."
**Substance:** Rinse, soak, strain, ferment, heat, filter. Shot at 120fps.
Minimal narration — the clouding water carries it.
**Format:** The `SHOT-LIST.md` beats. This doubles as brand film footage.

### 6. Yōjō · Behind the business · no product
**Hook:** "There's a Japanese word for what this brand is built on."
**Substance:** 養生 — yōjō, "nurturing life." A tradition of health cultivation
through consistency rather than intervention, written down in 1713. Gentle,
daily, sustained over decades. Contrast with an industry built on doing more to
your body faster.
**Note:** Never name a competitor or a competing ingredient category. The
restraint is the brand.

### 7. Why heat matters · Process · no product
**Hook:** "If you make rice water at home, you're missing this step."
**Substance:** Room-temperature fermentation is uncontrolled growth. Heating to
70–80 °C for fifteen minutes settles it before it goes anywhere near a bottle.
Genuinely useful to anyone doing the DIY version — which is most of the curly
community.
**Format:** Thermometer in the pot. High save rate.

# WEEK 3 — Ingredient authority

### 8. The yuzu decision · Education · no product
**Hook:** "There are two kinds of yuzu oil and only one belongs in a leave-in."
**Substance:** Cold-pressed citrus peel oil carries furanocoumarins, which are
phototoxic — a problem in something that sits on your hairline in daylight.
Steam distillation leaves them behind because they are too heavy to carry over.
Cold-pressed is the cheaper, more common one.
**CTA:** Quiz link.
**Note:** Highest-save post in the calendar. This is genuinely obscure and
genuinely matters.

### 9. pH · Process · no product
**Hook:** "If this number is wrong, the whole batch is wasted."
**Substance:** The strip, the colour, the target band. Explain that a
preservative only works inside a pH window, so this is not a detail — it is
whether the product survives.
**Format:** Macro on the strip changing colour.

### 10. Not adding the trendy thing · Contrarian · no product
**Hook:** "Everyone's stacking actives. I went the other way."
**Substance:** Rice water, camellia, yuzu — things people have used for a
thousand years, which is long enough to know exactly what they do. Nothing that
stings, nothing you cycle off, nothing you take a break from.
**Note:** Do not name the trend. "Ingredients with a thousand-year track record"
implies everything about the alternative and lets the viewer finish the thought.

# WEEK 4 — Show the work

### 11. The A/B test · Process · no product
**Hook:** "I made two versions of the same product to settle an argument."
**Substance:** One with konjac, one without, everything else identical. Explain
that you genuinely do not know whether 0.2% is perceptible, and that shipping an
ingredient you cannot feel is just a longer label.
**CTA:** "Which would you want? Quiz in bio."

### 12. What rice protein does · Education · no product
**Hook:** "This is the ingredient doing the actual work."
**Substance:** Hydrolyzed rice protein forms a light film on the strand — that
film is what holds a clump together. Not a coating, not a hold agent that
cracks. Explain why definition comes from film formation rather than stiffness.
**Format:** Carousel.

### 13. The packaging decision · Behind the business · no product
**Hook:** "I picked the plastic bottle. Here's the reasoning."
**Substance:** Glass looks premium and shatters in a wet bathroom. HDPE is
opaque, so it blocks the light that degrades citrus oil, and it costs less to
ship. Choosing the less glamorous option and explaining why builds more trust
than choosing the pretty one.

### 14. Cost transparency · Behind the business · no product
**Hook:** "Here's exactly what's in the bottle, in dollars."
**Substance:** Ingredient cost, bottle cost, label. Say the bottle costs more
than the liquid. People find this fascinating and almost nobody shows it.
**Note:** Only post real numbers. If you would not defend a figure, leave it out.

# WEEK 5 — First batch

### 15. The batch · Process · **needs product**
**Hook:** "First real batch."
**Substance:** Weighing, the konjac going in slowly, the fifteen-minute wait,
bottling. The payoff of the series so far.

### 16. The label · Education · **needs product**
**Hook:** "Every word on this label is there for a reason."
**Substance:** Walk the ingredient list in descending order. Explain that order
is by concentration, so you can read a label backwards to find out how little of
the hero ingredient is actually present. Genuinely useful literacy.
**Format:** Macro on the printed label. High save.

### 17. Camellia · Education · no product
**Hook:** "Japanese hair care has one oil, and it isn't argan."
**Substance:** Tsubaki — camellia seed oil. Roughly 80% oleic acid, with natural
squalene and vitamin E. Used for centuries, traditionally applied to damp hair.
Sets up the second product without selling it.

### 18. What went wrong · Behind the business · **needs product**
**Hook:** "This batch didn't work."
**Substance:** Whatever actually failed — clumped konjac, wrong pH, separation.
Show it. The failure post outperforms the success post almost every time, and it
is the one that makes the successes believable.

# WEEK 6 — Proof

### 19. Results · Proof · **needs product**
**Hook:** "Four days of using the thing I made."
**Substance:** Curls, on a head, damp to dry. Normal bathroom lighting, no
studio. This is the conversion asset — nothing else in the calendar sells like it.
**CTA:** Quiz link.

### 20. Application · Utility · **needs product**
**Hook:** "You're using leave-in wrong. Probably."
**Substance:** Damp not wet, scrunch don't comb, air dry or diffuse. Utility
content gets saved and shared to friends, and it positions you as someone who
knows hair rather than someone selling a bottle.

### 21. What's not in it · Contrarian · **needs product**
**Hook:** "The shortest ingredient list I could get away with."
**Substance:** What you removed and why — salt, camellia oil in this SKU, the
tagline that restated the descriptor. Every removal was a decision. Restraint as
a design philosophy.

### 22. Tester reactions · Social proof · **needs product**
**Hook:** "I gave it to people who don't have to be nice to me."
**Substance:** Real reactions, unedited, including lukewarm ones. A mixed review
you publish is worth more than five glowing ones.
**Note:** See `compliance/review-moderation.md` — testimonials on your own
channels are your claims. A tester saying it fixed a condition cannot be posted.

# WEEK 7 — Build toward launch

### 23. The number · Behind the business · **needs product**
**Hook:** "I can make exactly [N] bottles."
**Substance:** Hand-made means genuinely limited. Not a marketing device — an
actual constraint of one person and a scale. Real scarcity is rare and worth
stating plainly.
**CTA:** "Waitlist is the only way to get one. Quiz in bio."

### 24. Eight weeks · Story · **needs product**
**Hook:** "This took eight weeks and I'd do it again."
**Substance:** Compress the series into 30 seconds. Rice water to bottle. A brand
about patience proving it took its time — the medium agreeing with the message.

### 25. Label design · Behind the business · **needs product**
**Hook:** "I designed this label eleven times."
**Substance:** The mark was too small to read at arm's length. The Japanese type
had to match across every product. Show the versions. Craft content performs and
it reinforces the slow positioning.

### 26. Who it's for · Education · **needs product**
**Hook:** "This isn't for everyone and that's the point."
**Substance:** Who it suits and who it does not. Telling people not to buy is
the most trust-building thing a small brand can do, and it improves your reviews
by filtering out mismatched buyers.

---

# WEEK 8 — Founding batch

### 27. Open · Launch · **needs product**
**Hook:** "The first batch is open."
**Substance:** How many, what it costs, when it ships. Made after you order —
say that plainly, because the wait is the product, not an apology.

### 28. Close · Launch · **needs product**
**Hook:** "Last few."
**Substance:** Honest count. If it did not sell out, say that too and say what
you learned. Fake urgency is the fastest way to lose the trust the previous 27
posts built.

## Running it

**Batch-film.** One rice water session yields posts 1, 5, 7 and 9. One bottling
session yields 15, 16 and 18. Do not film daily.

**Post 3–4×/week.** Daily is not sustainable solo and quality drops faster than
frequency gains.

**Judge at post 20, not post 3.** The first ten underperform while the account
calibrates. That is normal and not a signal.

**Hook rule:** the first 1–2 seconds decide whether anything else is seen. Lead
with the claim, never the setup. "I took the salt out" — not "hi, I'm making a
haircare brand."

**Reuse the best.** A post that performs can be re-cut and posted again 6–8
weeks later. Almost nobody saw it the first time.

## If the formula slips

Weeks 1–4 are fourteen posts that need no product. If mixing is delayed, extend
the education and process pillars rather than posting a product you have not
made. Nothing in weeks 1–4 depends on anything physical existing.
